Before you decide.

How much does a website cost?

I have three packages, all priced in PKR. A single-page website is PKR 5,000 – 7,000, a multi-page website with basic SEO is PKR 8,000 – 10,000 and the full advanced SEO package with Google presence is PKR 22,000 – 25,000. The exact price within each range depends on your business and what you need.

Do I really work with you directly?

Yes. I'm Abdul Hafeez, an independent solo developer. You talk to the person who designs and builds your website — no agency layers and no hand-offs.

How do we get started, and what do I pay upfront?

Message me on WhatsApp with a short description of your business. Once we agree on the package, I start after a 50% advance. The remaining 50% is paid before final delivery. Full details are on the Terms page.

Will my website rank #1 on Google?

Nobody can honestly promise a specific ranking. What I do is build a fast, well-structured website with SEO fundamentals in place from day one — and in the Authority package, I go much deeper to improve your visibility on Google.

Can I see your real work first?

Of course. Every project on the Projects page is a live website — click any preview and the site opens. You can also read what my clients say on the Reviews page.
